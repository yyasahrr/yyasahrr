01 / A little about me
I’m Yashar Tavakolnia — a developer who enjoys working across the entire product, from experience design and frontend interactions to backend architecture, data, and deployment.

I care about the details that make software genuinely useful: clear UX, maintainable code, reliable behavior, and thoughtful trade-offs. I’m also exploring how AI and computer vision can power meaningful, human-centered technology.
